Analytics

Your ABA practice amasses a ton of essential data. But what’s more important is putting that data to work as actionable insights that achieve better business and client outcomes.

That’s why RethinkBH’s Analytics gives you powerful, intuitive dashboards that deliver relevant, real-time, and actionable insights. Our new Analytics features provide essential information for improving operational efficiency, decision making, clinical programming, treatment planning, and patient outcomes.

Our team of data scientists are also Board Certified Behavior Analysts who understand the challenges you face and the insights you need. The benefit is better care.

Surface Organizational Insights

See practice-wide insights on a visual dashboard so you can quickly determine what’s working and where you can improve authorization utilization and other business health indicators.

Focus on Client Progress and Outcomes

Help staff spend more time on the right programming and maintain a clear view of patient progress across their caseload. They can quickly and easily see clients in need of additional support.

Make More Informed Decisions More Quickly

Explore visualizations of essential practice data, including authorization utilization, service pacing, scheduling/staffing gaps and more.

Keep Your Practice Aligned

BCBAs and other key staff can also access practice management insights to see the connection between operational efficiency and improved care.

Key Features and Benefits

Clinical Insights Dashboard

  • Get a caseload-level view into each patient’s treatment data across therapists and locations, providing an overview of program effectiveness.

  • Anticipate crisis management training or treatment plan modification by identifying which therapists see patients with the most problem behaviors.

  • Quickly identify, analyze, and resolve barriers to patient progress, so necessary changes can be made to achieve an optimal outcome.

  • Deliver greater reporting on average days to mastery so BCBAs can better understand how long it takes to master programs and adapt to each child’s needs.

     

Practice Insights Dashboard

  • Focus on changes you can make to improve your business, for example, around service pacing, authorization utilization and operational efficiency.

  • See details on average weekly billed hours, which helps clinicians determine where they stand with authorized hours, service pacing, and scheduling.

  • Cancellation reporting makes rescheduling faster and triage easier if clients or staff miss appointments.

Frequently Asked Questions

How customizable are reports in RethinkBH?

Reports in RethinkBH are highly customizable. Providers can filter by client, staff, service type, and date range to generate insights that match their exact needs. Clinical outcome reports, billing summaries, and operational dashboards can be tailored for different audiences—whether administrators, clinicians, or payers—ensuring that practices always have the right level of detail.

How does RethinkBH provide data-driven insights for ABA practices?

RethinkBH offers expert-designed dashboards and data visualizations that provide clinical and business insights. Users have the flexibility to filter and organize this data to focus on what matters most to them.

What analytics does RethinkBH provide for clinical outcomes?

RethinkBH gives practices full visibility into clinical outcomes by tracking patient progress, goal mastery, and staff performance. The platform allows users to view data by day, providing detailed, daily insight into patient and staff performance to improve the quality of care.

What types of dashboards can be viewed with RethinkBH?

RethinkBH provides dashboards that offer views into clinical progress, billing and revenue, staff performance, and compliance tracking. These dashboards are designed to give users a comprehensive overview of key areas of their practice.
